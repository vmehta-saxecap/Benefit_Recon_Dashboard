Adoption, not workload, drove the growth

Total monthly tasks stayed roughly flat (1,033 in March, 997 in September) while tasks completed in the app rose from 305 to 801. The app now handles four of every five tasks.

The whole team is on the platform

All eight specialists show activity every month. Three of them account for roughly two thirds of volume; five exceed 400 completed tasks for the period.

Tasks close faster

Average elapsed time per completed task fell from 31d 2h to 11d 6h, the lowest of the period, while September volume was the second-highest. Elapsed time includes waiting.

MONTHLY TASK VOLUME
Four of every five September tasks were completed in the app, up from three in ten at go-live
Total reconciliation tasks per month and the portion completed in the app. Workload has been roughly stable (1,033 tasks in March, 997 in September); the share running through the platform rose from 30% to 80%. June was the heaviest month and the first in which the app handled the majority.
TEAM ADOPTION
All eight specialists work in the app; seven have completed tasks every month since go-live
Tasks completed in the app per specialist, March–September. The three most active account for 65% of volume and five of eight exceed 400 tasks. Volumes reflect assignment mix, not individual performance; the month-by-month view is in Appendix B.
CLIENTSPACE INTEGRATION
Case creation from the app stepped up in August and held in September
2,112 distinct ClientSpace cases were opened directly from the reconciliation workflow. The last two months produced 1,157 of them (55%); September’s 517 is 2.7× the March–July monthly average of 191.
TURNAROUND TIME
Tasks are closing faster: average elapsed time is down from 31 days to 11
Average elapsed time per task completed in the app, weighted by volume. September’s 11d 6h is the lowest of the period, 64% below March; the July–September average is 13d 8h. June closed the heaviest volume and a set of longer-running tasks. Elapsed time includes waiting, so it reflects throughput rather than hands-on effort.

APPENDIX G · DEFINITIONS
Scope and measurement limits
Sources are limited to the Dashboard and Audit Logs screens. All values reflect the October 9, 2026 review.

Sources

Dashboard → User Performance (completions, elapsed time, Notes and Cases), Dashboard → State Distribution and Open Past Due Date (monthly task totals, open and past-due tasks), Dashboard → Carriers Ranking, and Audit Logs → ClientSpace Tickets / Created and Activity by User. All screens viewed October 9, 2026.

Period and population

March–September 2026. March includes dates before the supplied March 10 go-live. User metrics cover eight specialists, including Kara Breedlove Johnson. mgarcia@gnapartners.com and Steviemarie Snyder are excluded. No start dates are assumed.

Task completions and participation

Completions are the sum of User Performance Completed. Recorded activity means any nonzero Completed, In Progress, Notes or Cases count. The selected-month attribution rule is not exposed. Counts do not establish login frequency or active days.

ClientSpace creations

Area = ClientSpace Tickets and Type = Created. All pages reviewed and ticket numbers deduplicated within and across months. Counts follow audit event dates and include all actors: 2,106 specialist creations and 6 other or system creations.

Turnaround time

Completed-volume-weighted average of each user’s displayed average elapsed time, using rounded displayed values. Elapsed time includes waiting and does not measure hands-on effort.

Share completed in the app

Monthly app completions divided by the dashboard’s displayed task total for the same month. The two series come from different screens; the share is an indicator of how much of the displayed workload ran through the app, not an audited reconciliation of task populations. Historical workload views are current snapshots.

Remaining limits

Financial return, processing success, support ownership and formal project acceptance are not established by these screens.
